Build a complete, polished, responsive **personal crochet product showcase website with an admin panel**.

The website is for a person who makes and sells handmade crochet/yarn creations such as stuffed toys, clothes, bags, flowers, accessories, keychains, and other handmade products.

## IMPORTANT BUSINESS MODEL

This is **NOT a traditional e-commerce website**.

There must be:

* ❌ No shopping cart
* ❌ No checkout
* ❌ No online payment system
* ❌ No Buy Now button
* ❌ No customer account system
* ❌ No built-in ordering/payment processing

Instead, visitors simply browse the products and contact the owner if they are interested in purchasing something.

Every product should have an **"Inquire" / "Contact"** button.

The owner will provide their actual contact links later.

---

# 1. OVERALL DESIGN STYLE

The website should have a strong:

**Cute + Girly + Pink + Feminine + Handmade Crochet + Cozy + Elegant**

aesthetic.

Think of a combination of:

🎀 cute pink boutique
🧶 cozy crochet craft room
🌸 handmade gift shop
💕 feminine personal brand
✨ elegant scrapbook

The website should feel intentionally designed, not like a generic AI-generated template.

The crochet products themselves must remain the main visual focus.

---

# 2. COLOR PALETTE

Use a soft feminine palette.

Primary colors:

* Baby pink
* Blush pink
* Pastel rose
* Dusty pink
* Soft cream
* Ivory
* White

Use a slightly darker pink for:

* Buttons
* Important labels
* Prices
* Active navigation
* Highlights

Avoid:

* Neon pink
* Extremely saturated colors
* Too many unrelated colors

The overall appearance should be soft and pleasant.

---

# 3. GIRLY DECORATIVE ELEMENTS

Use subtle decorative elements inspired by:

* 🎀 Ribbons
* 🌸 Flowers
* Hearts
* Tiny sparkles
* Yarn
* Crochet stitches
* Small bows
* Floral patterns
* Decorative dots

Prefer actual SVG/CSS decorative elements or tasteful graphics instead of relying heavily on emoji.

## Ribbon/Bow Elements

Use cute fabric-style bows throughout the design.

Possible locations:

* Beside the logo
* Around section headings
* On featured product cards
* As section separators
* Near buttons
* Around decorative areas
* In empty spaces

Do not put a bow everywhere.

Decorations should feel naturally placed and varied.

---

# 4. BACKGROUND

Use a soft cream, ivory, or very light pink base.

Add very subtle decorative patterns such as:

* Tiny hearts
* Small flowers
* Dots
* Crochet stitch patterns
* Yarn-inspired lines

Keep the patterns low contrast.

They should enhance the design without making text difficult to read.

---

# 5. TYPOGRAPHY

Use two complementary fonts.

## Headings

Use a feminine, elegant, slightly handwritten or soft decorative font.

## Body

Use a clean and highly readable font.

Do NOT use an overly decorative font for paragraphs, prices, or forms.

Product information must remain easy to read.

# 16. INQUIRE / CONTACT SYSTEM

This is one of the most important features.

There is NO checkout.

Instead, each product has:

**♡ Inquire**

or:

**Contact About This Product**

When clicked, show the available contact methods configured by the admin.

For example:

* Messenger
* Facebook
* Instagram
* Email
* Phone
* Other

The admin will enter the actual links.

If technically practical, clicking Inquire for a product should prepare a message containing the product name.

For example:

"I'm interested in the Crochet Bunny Plushie."

Then direct the visitor to the selected contact method.

Do not build an internal messaging system.

Do not process payments.

# FINAL QUALITY TARGET

Do not stop at a basic functional prototype.

Make the result feel like a **real, polished personal crochet brand website** that the owner could actually show to customers.

The website should communicate:

**"Everything here is handmade with care."**

The public side should prioritize **beauty and browsing**.

The admin side should prioritize **simplicity and product management**.

The final result should be a **cute, pink, feminine crochet showcase website with a powerful but easy-to-use admin panel**, while intentionally avoiding traditional e-commerce checkout functionality.
